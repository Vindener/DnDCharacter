## English version

### Mythgate 5e Companion — Terms of Service

**1. About the app**

Mythgate 5e Companion is an unofficial D&D 5e companion app built on the
System Reference Document 5.1 (SRD 5.1) under the Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 license. The app is not a Wizards of the Coast product and
does not use their trademarks or logos. Full attribution text is available
in-app: **Settings → Legal & Licenses**.

The app is **local-first**: creating and editing a character works fully
offline and without an account. Data on your device is yours and is stored
locally regardless of cloud sync.

**2. Account**

Google Sign-In is optional and only required for cloud sync and sharing
character sheets. By signing in, you confirm the information provided (name,
email, profile photo from Google) is accurate.

**3. Cloud sync and sharing**

A character sheet can have multiple editors (owner + editors), invited
**only by email** — there is no public search or user listing. If you play
in a campaign with a DM, the DM may see your character's change history
(`changeHistory`) — this is needed to track in-game events and is disclosed
in the Privacy Policy.

You are responsible for who you invite as an editor on your own sheet.
Removing yourself from another user's sheet as an editor is available at any
time via Settings.

**4. Acceptable use**

By using sharing features (inviting editors, DM notes, campaigns), you agree
not to:
- use text fields (notes, homebrew descriptions) for illegal, abusive
  content, or to harass other users;
- attempt to access sheets or campaigns you have not been invited to;
- disregard that co-editors of a sheet may be editing at the same time —
  conflicts are resolved by the app, not by one-sided overwrites of another
  person's data.

**5. No warranty**

The app is provided "as is." Local-first mode is the primary safeguard
against data loss: characters created without signing in always remain on
your device. For cloud sync, the developer makes reasonable efforts to
preserve data but does not guarantee against sync failures or temporary
Firebase infrastructure unavailability.

**6. Account deletion**

Your account and associated data are deleted via **Settings → Account →
Danger zone → Delete account**, or by request via the account-deletion page
(linked from the Privacy Policy). Shared sheets do not disappear for other
editors — details in `docs/privacy-policy.en.md`.

**7. Changes to these terms**

These terms may be updated. Material changes (e.g., introducing
monetization) will be reflected here and in the release changelog.

**8. Contact**

Questions, reports about another user's content, deletion requests —
**mythgate5e@gmail.com**.
